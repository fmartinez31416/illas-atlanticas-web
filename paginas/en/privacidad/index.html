Privacy policy · Illas Atlánticas Ático, Aguiño

What data we process when you book, why, how long we keep it, and how to exercise your GDPR rights.

1. Data controller. Fernando Martínez Piñeiro, owner of the tourist accommodation Illas Atlánticas Ático (VUT-CO-007656), Rúa Francisco Lorenzo Mariño 93, Aguiño, 15960 Ribeira (A Coruña), Spain. Contact: info@illasatlanticasatico.es.

2. Data we process and purpose. When you book or contact us we process your name, phone, email and the data required by Spanish tourism law for the traveller register (ID, date of birth, nationality, signature), reported to the police by legal obligation (Royal Decree 933/2021). We use them only to manage your booking and stay.

3. Legal basis. Performance of the lodging contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR), compliance with legal obligations (art. 6.1.c GDPR) and, where given, consent.

4. Retention. Booking data is kept as long as tax obligations require (up to 5 years) and traveller registers 3 years. Then deleted.

5. Recipients. No data is sold or shared with third parties, except processors (booking platform, payment channel) and mandatory legal disclosures (traveller register).

6. Your rights. Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability by emailing info@illasatlanticasatico.es. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

7. Cookies and measurement. This website uses only strictly necessary technical cookies and aggregated audience measurement that does not identify individuals.
