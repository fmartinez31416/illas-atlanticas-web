The Índice G aggregates the public scores of Peñín, Tim Atkin, Wine Advocate, James Suckling and Decanter across the five Galician denominations — Rías Baixas, Ribeiro, Ribeira Sacra, Monterrei and Valdeorras — with a reproducible method: G = P̂ − 1.5·σ. Bands: Exceptional ≥ 92 · Notable ≥ 88 · Very good ≥ 84 · Good ≥ 80. Every note links to its public listing.
